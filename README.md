## About

I work where AI systems meet the ecosystems around them: the agents, platforms, publishers and developers that have to trust one another for any of it to work.

By day I am a Product and Ecosystem Leader in AI Ecosystems. Outside of it I build **[Korext](https://korext.com)**, a governance runtime for AI generated code, and lead **[Korext Open Source](https://oss.korext.com)**, seven open standards that make AI written software traceable, licensable and accountable.
